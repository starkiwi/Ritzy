<h1>Ritzy</h1>
<h3><strong>A Stealthy Minigame</strong></h3>
<p>Ritzy is a strange little creature whose mission is to escape the heavily guarded compound she is trapped in. In this maze, stealth is the key. Stay hidden from the guards, and you may just find your way out.&nbsp;</p>
<p>Game created for <a target="_blank" href="https://itch.io/jam/tavern-game-jam-autumn26">The Artists Tavern Game Jam - Autumn '26</a>&nbsp;with the theme '<strong>IN THE SHADOWS</strong>'.</p>
